Kind: feature

- **Knows where a conversation is placed from Kehikot itself.** The epic, parts and kehikko a job or review belongs to are now asked of Kehikot, instead of read from another module's files. A conversation Kehikot has not placed is unplaced, as before.
- **Reads your checklists as data.** With a checklist module that offers it, the checklists held against a change are read as data rather than from their printed form.
- **Names the module that shows pages by its own name.** A builder or reviewer is told it may look at the running app through the module on your kehikko that shows pages, by that module's name and tool — and is told nothing of it when there is none.
- **Says where your draft is.** A review card waiting for you says the name of the module that holds the draft.

## For agents

- The conversation's place comes from the host method `sessions.placeOf` (capability `sessions:place`, protocol 2.85.0).
- `/review-factory` needs `{ service: 'review.drafts' }`: a kehikko with any module providing that service.
- Checklists are read with `format: 'json'` from a `checklist.holder` whose descriptor has `formats: ['json']`; the print is read only from one without.
- Factory provides `module.folders` for `.kehikot/factory/`.
