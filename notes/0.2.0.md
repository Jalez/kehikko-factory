- **One sentence in, a reviewed change out.** Type `/factory` and what you want in Claude Code, and a line of agents writes the spec, plans the reviews, builds it on its own branch and tests it, without touching your checkout.
- **Reviews that fit the change.** A planner picks the review angles this change needs, such as security, accessibility or data migration; you can add or remove them before reviews start.
- **Argued, then judged.** Each review that asks for changes is answered by a counter-argument, and a judge rules which points are required before the change goes back for at most two more rounds.
- **You have the last word.** Every job ends with you approving and merging it, or sending it back with a note, unless you let the judge merge approved jobs in that project.
- **Watch it on the board.** A Factory container shows the epic's jobs as cards moving from Spec to Merged, with what each one is doing right now.
